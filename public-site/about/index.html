About QEV

QEV is Qira LLC’s encryption vault and evidence platform: portable vaults, optional capture gateway, signed proof.

QEV (Qira Encryption Vault) is a product of Qira LLC. It seals work evidence into packages that open with your keys and can carry signed proof a third party can check.

What we sell / offer

  • Free vault tools — browser, desktop, CLI for offline sealed files
  • Team pilot — customer-controlled capture gateway + connectors
  • Trust program — scoped certificates, not vague badges

History

Earlier materials used the research name BRY-NFET-SX. The commercial product name is QEV. Crypto remains standard AEAD — not a novel cipher.
